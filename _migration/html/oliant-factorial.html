Frequently Asked Questions

Find out more about Factorial’s ongoing evolution and services. Don't see your question here? Get in touch  with us, and we'll help you find what you are looking for.
What is Factorial?

Factorial is an HR software company dedicated to helping businesses of all sizes realize their full potential by automating their HR processes. This frees up time wasted on manual administrative tasks and enables them to maximize productivity within the company.

Factorial was founded in 2019 and has since achieved 300% growth, attaining tech unicorn status after securing a $120 million Series C investment round.

What does Factorial do?

Factorial's HR software is a centralized platform that can manage all HR operations. This includes time, shift and performance tracking, recruiting, onboarding and training, document administration, scheduling and payroll.

Factorial HR Software is part Human Resource Information System (HRIS), part Human Capital Management System (HCM), and part Human Resource Management System (HRMS).

If you find yourself short of time due to a mountain of manual administrative tasks, Factorial will help you stay organized and productive by automating your HR department with the help of a single, centralized operational hub.

Is Factorial safe?

Factorial is ISO/IEC 27001:2017 certified. This certification represents the highest level of the global information security standard available today, providing customers with assurance that they meet rigorous international standards on security.

As of December 2023, Factorial holds a SOC2 Type II report, which examines a service provider's internal controls and systems related to security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy of data. This is according to the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) Trust Service Criteria.

What is a Human Resource Information System (HRIS)?

Human Resource Information System, or HRIS, is a comprehensive software solution designed to streamline and manage various HR processes.

HRIS typically includes features for employee data management, benefits administration, payroll processing, time and attendance tracking, and other essential HR functions. By centralizing data and automating routine tasks, HRIS enables organizations to enhance efficiency, accuracy, and overall workforce management.

What is Human Capital Management (HCM)?

HCM stands for Human Capital Management. It is a cloud-based solution that encompasses various tasks associated with talent management within a company.
‍
HCM incorporates features such as performance tracking, learning and development, and workforce planning. HCM is designed to optimize the overall employee experience, through tools such as artificial intelligence and digital assistants to facilitate collaboration and decision-making.

What is a Human Resource Management System (HRMS)?

Human Resource Management System (HRMS) is a system that includes modules for employee data management, payroll, recruitment, benefits administration, training, and more.

The term is sometimes used interchangeably with HCM, reflecting the comprehensive nature of the software suite aimed at effectively managing an organization's human resources.
