Our client's users needed to email their contacts without leaving the
platform, and then copy replies back into it by hand, which was slow and
error-prone. We built a Gmail integration that lets them start
conversations from the app's own interface, using their own Google
Workspace accounts. Every reply, forward and new participant on those
threads is then captured, analyzed and saved automatically, with no extra
work.

**Privacy by design.** Users connect their Gmail account once, through
Google-verified OAuth with sensitive-scope permissions. The app never
reads the inbox: it only follows threads the user started through the
app, and ignores everything else.
